**Formula Student · Engineering · Cross-disciplinary collaboration**

I am part of Helix NMBU, the Formula Student team at the Norwegian University of Life Sciences. We design, build and compete with our own race car. The team brings together students from several disciplines to work on technical solutions, manufacturing, finance and organisation.

## What is Formula Student?

Formula Student is an international engineering competition for student teams. We develop a car from idea to finished vehicle, and must justify the design, document manufacturing and costs, and present a business plan. The car also undergoes technical inspections before it can take part in driving events.

## My role in Helix

### 2024–2025: Member of the electronics department

During the 2024–2025 season, I was a member of the electronics department. I started with data collection and later contributed to several parts of the car's development, including electronics, wiring diagrams and brake disc work.

### 2025–2026: Head of electronics and board member

During the 2025–2026 season, I was Head of the Electronics Department and a board member at Helix NMBU. I was responsible for the department's technical direction, from design choices and decisions to planning and carrying out key parts of the Formula Student car's development and build work.

### Data collection and electronics

I worked with data collection using **Telemetry Extractor** and **Telemetry Overlay**. I used these tools to extract and visualise vehicle data alongside video footage, making it possible to review GPS position, speed and acceleration during a test run.

![Visualisation of test data from the Helix car in Telemetry Extractor](/helix-telemetry.png)

By combining video, driving route and measurement data, I could investigate the car's behaviour across different parts of the test area. The visualisation made the data easier to interpret and use in further development.

I was also responsible for the car's electronics, including the shutdown circuit, wiring to the ECU and motor control. The shutdown circuit is a safety system that switches off the car if the system detects a fault.

### Brake disc design

I also worked on the brake discs. I designed the brake discs and carried out thermal tests in Fusion 360 to investigate how they would handle the heat generated during braking. The tests were used to assess and improve the design before production.

## Nordic Test Event 2026

I took part in Nordic Test Event (NTE) in 2026. NTE is a Nordic test event for student teams, where we can prepare the car for the summer season through technical inspection, testing and collaboration with other teams.

We did not have a finished car when we arrived at NTE, but that worked well because the event was for testing rather than competition. The car went through scrutineering, where we received white notes for items that could be faulty or needed improvement. This gave us clear feedback on what still needed to be completed.

After the inspection, we used the experience from NTE to complete the car's wiring harness. It was an important next step in the electronics work and in preparing the car.
